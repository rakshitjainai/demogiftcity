Question
What is a Registered FME – Non-Retail?
Take a moment. Structure your answer as if the interviewer is in front of you.
Interview-ready answer

A Registered FME–Non-Retail is an FME category under the IFSCA fund management framework that is permitted to manage non-retail schemes subject to the applicable eligibility, governance, investment and compliance requirements.

Likely follow-up: How is this different from a Registered FME–Retail?
The preparation gap

The interview may be tomorrow. The knowledge behind it can take months.

To genuinely prepare for an FME role, you'd have to navigate IFSCA Regulations, guidelines and circulars, fund and scheme structures, PPM and documentation, AML/CFT/KYC, FEMA, reporting, valuation, enforcement and practical cases.

The problem isn't that the information isn't available. It's knowing what to learn, how deeply to learn it, and whether you'll be able to recall it when the interviewer asks.
